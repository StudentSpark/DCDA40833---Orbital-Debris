Conclusion

This project investigated the issues surrounding orbital debris, the potential for Kessler Syndrome, and the current and future developments of near-Earth space. While for many, space is a place restricted to textbooks and television, operations in space actually play a massive and increasingly important role in daily life. As such, many seemingly distant issues in space can have very real impacts on the day-to-day lives of people everywhere, such as GPS satellites, internet communication, and critical maritime and aviation communication infrastructure. This research also uncovered several potential avenues for further research and study, such as the effectiveness and practicality of space debris removal technologies and their increasing importance as Earth’s orbital environment continues to be developed, and further preventative measures of orbital debris. Additional investigation into orbital mechanics, astrophysics, and aerospace engineering would provide in-depth context into the specific nature of the issues, but were largely unnecessary for the purposes of this project.
